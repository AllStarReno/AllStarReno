A tub-to-shower conversion is one of the most requested bathroom upgrades in Conway and Central Arkansas. Many homeowners no longer use their bathtub and prefer the convenience, safety, and modern look of a walk-in shower. All-Star Renovations specializes in clean, efficient conversions using our All-Star Shower System.

Benefits of Converting a Tub to a Shower

  • ✓ Safer step-in access instead of climbing over a tub wall
  • ✓ Easier cleaning with grout-free wall systems
  • ✓ Updated, more modern bathroom appearance
  • ✓ Better use of space in smaller bathrooms
  • ✓ Most conversions completed in 1–2 days

What the Conversion Includes

We remove the existing bathtub, prepare the wet area, and install a complete All-Star Shower System. This typically includes a solid composite stone base, waterproof wall panels, coordinated fixtures, and professional finishing. Final scope and pricing are confirmed during your free in-home consultation.
