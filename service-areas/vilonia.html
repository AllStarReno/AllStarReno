Why Traditional Tile & Cheap Acrylic Fail in Vilonia & East Faulkner County

Between rural wells across Hwy 64 and mineral-dense local supply, Vilonia bathrooms require specialized commercial-grade composite stone surfaces that withstand iron, sulfur, and heavy sediment.

The Well Water & Mineral Threat

Homes in rural Vilonia, Naylor, and Cypress Valley battle high iron concentrations and heavy calcium scale. Traditional bathrooms degrade rapidly:

  • ✕ Porous Tile Grout: Soaks up iron oxide within months, resulting in permanently orange, mold-harboring joints that require acidic scrubbing.
  • ✕ Thin 0.125" Acrylic Liners: Trap sulfur gases and micro-cracks under boiling water cycles, turning brittle, yellowish, and soft at the base.
  • ✕ Permanent Chemical Etching: Abrasive cleansers strip factory glaze, accelerating future rust accumulation.
Result: Premature failure in 3–5 years Costly Replacement Cycle

The Rigid Core Board (RCB) Defense

All-Star Renovations installs ultra-dense Rigid Core Board composite stone panels specifically calibrated for Central Arkansas well profiles:

  • ✓ Zero-Porosity Surface: Iron and red mud deposits rest on the outer shield without penetrating. Wipes 100% clean with mild soap and a squeegee in 45 seconds.
  • ✓ Grout-Free Interlocking Panels: Eliminates mold and mildew vectors entirely with moisture-locked micro-joints.
  • ✓ Solid Density Impact Resistance: Solid stone-composite core resists livestock equipment scratches, heavy footwork, and dropping brass hardware.
