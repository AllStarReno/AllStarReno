How quickly can I get a walk-in shower installed in Little Rock?

expand_more

Can historic Little Rock bathrooms in The Heights or Hillcrest be converted?

expand_more
Absolutely. Homes built between 1920 and 1950 often feature narrow door clearances, uneven lath-and-plaster, and aged cast-iron drain assemblies. Licensed Contractor Thomas Pedersen personally surveys historic Hillcrest and Heights residences to formulate structural subfloor reinforcement, customized low-profile shower pans, and authentic aesthetics that respect the architectural character of the home.
