How much does a tub-to-shower conversion cost in Maumelle, AR? expand_more

Tub-to-shower conversions in Maumelle typically range from $5,000 to $12,500+ depending on system scope and materials:
  • Premier Tier ($5,000 – $7,499): Standard 60x32 alcove tub replacement with durable non-porous composite walls and chrome fixtures.
  • Signature Tier ($7,500 – $9,999): Our most common Maumelle installation. Custom-fit Rigid Core Stone slabs, sliding frameless bypass roller glass, and built-in recessed shampoo niches.
  • Designer Walk-In / ADA ($10,000 – $12,500+): Full 1990s platform tub demolition, subfloor leveling, zero-threshold entry, grab bars, and dual-head thermostatic valves.

How long does a bathroom remodel take in Maumelle? expand_more

Most wet-area conversions are completely installed and operational in 1 to 2 business days (24 to 48 hours). Because our master craftsmen precut your Rigid Core Stone composite panels using digital precision measurements prior to arrival, demo starts Wednesday morning and you can shower Thursday afternoon.

Can All-Star Renovations remove large 1990s jetted platform tubs? expand_more

Yes, this is our signature specialization in Maumelle neighborhoods like Country Club of Arkansas and Edgewood. We handle full electrical disconnect of old whirlpool pumps, remove the tile framework, inspect and reinforce the underlying subfloor against dry rot, relocate the drain to 2-inch standard code, and expand the usable shower space dramatically.
