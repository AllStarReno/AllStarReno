Why Sherwood Homeowners Are Retiring Grout & Acrylic Liners

Central Arkansas humidity and municipal water minerals naturally attack traditional cement grout and cheap glued acrylic liners. In vintage Sherwood subdivisions, settling foundations cause caulk joint cracking, inviting moisture into wood subfloors.

cancel

Failing Caulk & Mildew: Grout is porous concrete; in warm Arkansas climates, black mold colonizes inside the matrix within 18 months.

Acrylic "Bath In A Day" Traps: Franchise slip-over liners trap trapped humidity beneath, rotting drywall and releasing chemical odors in heated showers.

All-Star Rigid Core Board (RCB): 100% non-porous composite stone panels anchored directly to studs, eliminating grout joints and backing rot forever.
