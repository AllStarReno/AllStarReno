Why Cabot Homeowners Are Ditching Traditional Tile & Grout

Lonoke County municipal water and surrounding rural wells carry dissolved calcium, magnesium, and iron. When water evaporates on standard ceramic grout, these minerals bond inside microscopic cement pores, creating permanent discoloration and persistent black mold.

Our 100% solid composite panels have a zero-porosity surface. Calcium wipes off with a microfiber towel and warm water. No bleach, no acidic scrubbers, and zero resealing over the lifetime of your Cabot home.
