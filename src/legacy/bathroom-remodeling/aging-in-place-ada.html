Frequently Asked Questions

Accessible Bathroom Voice-Search Answers

Clear, candid answers regarding insurance, project speed, and aesthetic styling.

Does Medicare cover walk-in tubs in Arkansas?

expand_more
Original Medicare (Part A and B) typically categorizes walk-in tubs and barrier-free showers as personal comfort or convenience items rather than primary Durable Medical Equipment (DME), meaning direct out-of-pocket coverage is rare. However, Arkansas seniors frequently utilize Medicare Advantage (Part C) specialized wellness stipends, VA Specially Adapted Housing (SAH/SHA) grants for qualifying veterans, or dedicated fixed-rate home improvement financing through HFS and Acorn Finance.

How quickly can an accessible roll-in shower be installed?

expand_more
At All-Star Renovations, our specialized installation team completes most seamless zero-threshold roll-in conversions within 1 to 2 consecutive days. We arrive with precision pre-measured materials, tear out the high-walled tub on morning one, rebuild the water barrier and backing, and install the finished composite walls and safety bars on day two.

Can grab bars be installed without looking like a hospital room?

expand_more
Absolutely. We exclusively install architectural grade, ADA-compliant safety bars (rated to 500 lbs load capacity) that blend seamlessly with high-end designer fixtures. Choose from brushed bronze, matte black, and satin nickel with multi-function integrations such as combination towel bars, corner soap shelves, and vertical shampoo ledges.

Is a zero-threshold curbless shower wheelchair-accessible?

expand_more
Yes. Our roll-in showers are engineered with a smooth zero-degree transition from your exterior floor, eliminating any raised lip. They accommodate standard manual and electric wheelchairs, shower transfer chairs, and walkers with linear channel drainage that stops water from escaping into the dry room.
