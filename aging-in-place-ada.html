People Also Ask

What makes a bathroom aging-in-place friendly?

Low or zero thresholds, grab bars, slip-resistant surfaces, easy-to-use fixtures, and good lighting.

Can you add grab bars to an existing shower?

Yes, and we can reinforce mounting points during a remodel for long-term safety.
