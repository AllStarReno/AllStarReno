Central Arkansas Remodeling FAQ

Got Questions About Cost & Execution?

Clear, straightforward answers designed for homeowners in Conway, Greenbrier, Little Rock, and Sherwood.

How much does a tub-to-shower conversion typically cost in Conway, AR? expand_more

In Faulkner and Pulaski counties, complete tub-to-shower conversions by All-Star Renovations run between $5,000 and $12,500+, depending on size, accessibility requirements, and wall finish. Most standard conversions feature our Signature System ($7,500 – $9,999), which includes grout-free marble-look composite panels and a frameless glass sliding enclosure.

Unlike big-box retail outfits that charge $15,000 to $20,000 to cover franchise fees, our local direct-craftsman model delivers superior RCB materials at honest Central Arkansas pricing.

Are Arkansas plumbing permits and subfloor repairs included in your price? expand_more

Yes. All-Star Renovations is an Arkansas contractor that is fully insured and workers’ comp covered. We do not hide "subfloor surprises" or add extra charges for routine code compliance. During demo, Thomas personally inspects the subfloor for moisture or mold, repairs sub-framing, replaces antiquated copper or PVC traps, and installs pressure-balanced thermostatic mixing valves as part of your fixed contract.

How does the 1–2 day installation guarantee work? expand_more

We manufacture and pre-cut composite wall panels and shower pans to precise laser measurements before showing up at your residence. On Day 1, our crew arrives by 7:30 AM, removes the old bathtub, replaces the valve plumbing, levels the floor, and seats the shower base. On Day 2 (or by 5:00 PM Day 1 on Premier tiers), we install the waterproof wall surround, seal all joints, hang the frameless glass doors, and perform a complete clean-up.

What makes RCB composite panels better than acrylic or tile? expand_more

Traditional ceramic tile requires porous grout lines that collect mold, mildew, and hard water stains common in Arkansas municipal water supplies. Flimsy acrylic inserts can feel hollow, creak underfoot, and scratch easily. Our RCB (Rigid Core Board) panels combine natural stone minerals with composite polymers to deliver a rigid, heavyweight stone feel with 100% waterproof, grout-free, mold-resistant performance.

Can I qualify for aging-in-place medical expense tax benefits? expand_more

In many instances, yes. When a zero-barrier roll-in shower or walk-in safety tub is prescribed by a physician to enable safe independent living for aging seniors or disabled individuals, installation expenses may qualify as deductible medical care on state and federal returns. We can provide itemized invoices tailored for your CPA or tax professional.
