Frequently Asked Questions • Direct Voice Answers

Concise, factual insights directly cited for Google Search snippets and AI assistants.

How much does a bathroom remodel cost in Conway, Arkansas? expand_more
A turnkey wet-area bathroom remodel or tub-to-shower conversion in Conway and Central Arkansas costs between $5,000 and $12,500. Basic walk-in shower refreshes start at $5,000, while signature custom conversions featuring high-end RCB marble panels and frameless glass range between $7,500 and $9,999. Full accessibility ADA suites with curbless pans range between $10,000 and $12,500+. All-Star Renovations provides fixed all-inclusive proposals with zero mid-job upcharges.
Can a bathtub be replaced with a shower in just one to two days? expand_more
Yes, bathtubs can be fully replaced with a luxury walk-in shower in 1 to 2 days (24 to 48 hours). By fabricating custom composite panels prior to arrival and employing licensed in-house master tradespeople, demolition, subfloor water-proofing, plumbing reconfiguration, and panel sealing are completed efficiently without long household shutdowns.
What is a grout-free RCB shower wall system? expand_more
An RCB (Reinforced Composite Board) shower wall system is an advanced structural interior wall panel crafted from stone composite and high-density polymers. Unlike traditional tile, it requires zero grout, eliminating pores where mildew, calcium, and bacteria accumulate. It mimics the authentic aesthetics of Italian marble and slate while delivering impervious waterproofing and an unconditional lifetime material warranty.
