    Frequently Asked Questions

    Everything you need to know about our renovation process, pricing, and financing options.

    How long does a typical bathroom remodel take?
    Most of our bathroom remodeling projects, including shower replacements and tub-to-shower conversions, are completed in just 1 to 2 days. We prioritize efficiency without compromising on quality craftsmanship.
    Do you offer financing options?
    Yes. Financing is available through Acorn Finance, HFS Financial and RenoFi. You can apply for pre-qualification through the lenders using the links on this page. Rates, terms and approval are set by the lenders.
    How much does a bathroom remodel cost?
    Pricing varies depending on the scope of the project, materials selected, and specific requirements like accessibility features. We offer free, no-obligation in-home consultations where we review your project and provide a quote.
    Are your materials mold and mildew resistant?
    We use durable, non-porous materials designed to resist mold and mildew, making your new bathroom easy to clean and maintain.
    Do you provide a warranty for your work?
    Yes. All-Star Shower Systems materials carry a lifetime product warranty, and our installation work is backed by a 5-year workmanship warranty. Every project is also covered by our 100% Customer Guarantee.
    What services does All-Star Renovations offer?
    We offer bathroom remodeling (including All-Star Shower Systems, tub-to-shower conversions, walk-in showers, walk-in tubs and aging-in-place bathrooms), kitchen remodeling and handyman services.
    Are you licensed and insured?
    Yes. All-Star Renovations is a fully insured, workers’ comp covered Arkansas contractor. We have been serving Central Arkansas since 2014.
    Where do you work?
    We are based in Conway, AR and serve a 50-mile radius of Conway, including Faulkner, Pulaski, Saline and Lonoke counties.
    What are your business hours?
    Monday to Friday 7:30 AM to 5:00 PM, Saturday 7:00 AM to 7:00 PM and Sunday 9:00 AM to 3:00 PM.
