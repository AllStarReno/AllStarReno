How can a full remodel take only 1 to 2 days?

expand_more
We custom precision-fabricate all wall panels, shower bases, and glass systems prior to arriving at your home. When our master crew arrives, demolition, subfloor preparation, plumbing rough-in, and installation happen seamlessly without multi-week wait times or subcontractor handoffs.

What makes RCB panels superior to acrylic or fiberglass?

expand_more
RCB (Reinforced Composite Board) is engineered with real mineral stone dust and reinforced resins. It will not flex, yellow, or crack like cheap builder-grade acrylic. It gives you the solid visual density and tactile cold-stone feel of Italian marble without the water absorption or porous grout lines.
