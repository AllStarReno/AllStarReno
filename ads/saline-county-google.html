Can you genuinely finish a complete shower remodel in 1 to 2 days? expand_more
Yes. Because our rigid stone composite panels are engineered and precision pre-cut to your shower's exact dimensions prior to demo day, we do not require days of messy wet tile floating or curing times. Demolition, subfloor waterproofing, plumbing updates, and panel installation are fully completed in 24 to 36 hours.
Do you charge travel fees or mileage from Conway to Benton or Bryant? expand_more
Never. Saline County is one of our most active daily service territories via I-430 and I-30. You pay the exact same transparent rates in Hurricane Lake Estates, Salem, or Longhills as a customer in downtown Conway, with $0 transit or trip charges.
How does rigid stone composite handle Saline County tap and well water? expand_more
Unlike porous cement grout that absorbs iron and calcium deposits from local water supplies, our rigid core board (RCB) stone composite has zero porosity. Water sheets off cleanly, resisting soap scum and mineral staining. A quick wipe with a microfiber cloth and warm water keeps it pristine for decades.
