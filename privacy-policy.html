1. Information We Collect

All-Star Renovations collects only the pertinent residential data necessary to calculate precise remodeling estimates, conduct in-home laser measurement consultations, schedule wet-area installations, and provide post-installation lifetime material guarantees throughout Central Arkansas.

badge Contact & Identity Identifiers

Full legal name, primary telephone number, email address, and home physical service address where wet-space conversions, ADA walk-in showers, or kitchen transformations are scheduled.

home_work Home & Structural Specifications

Bathroom dimensions, subfloor status (slab vs. crawlspace), plumbing drain alignments, photographs submitted for remote pre-assessments, age of structure, and accessibility accommodation requests (e.g., zero-threshold entries, grab bar reinforcements, seating).

chat_bubble_outline Interactive Digital Dialogues

Transcripts and selections gathered through our interactive embedded Swivl quoting assistant, direct telephone consultations, appointment scheduler inputs, and inbound text communications.

2. How We Use Your Information

We limit the processing of your information strictly to operational construction deliverables, project logistics, and explicit customer requests:

  • check_circle Delivering Architectural Estimates: Calculating custom dimensional quotes for tub-to-shower conversions, barrier-free walk-ins, and full interior renovations.
  • check_circle Fulfilling 1–2 Day Rapid Installations: Coordinating in-house craftsmen, staging plumbing materials, securing city permits in Conway or Faulkner County, and executing zero-delay site work.
  • check_circle Facilitating Flexible Financing: Transmitting your application strictly with authorized, bank-grade lending networks (HFS Financial and Acorn Finance) solely upon your direct direction and request.
  • check_circle Warranty & Service Records: Maintaining structural lifetime warranties and serial records for acrylic, composite wall panels, and Moen/Kohler fixtures installed in your home.

7. SMS & Phone Communication Disclosure (A2P 10DLC)

By supplying your phone number through an instant quote form, consultation scheduler, or text conversation, you agree that All-Star Renovations may contact you via voice call or SMS text message to coordinate your project.

Standard SMS Guidelines & Opt-Out Provisions:
  • Message Purpose: Consultation schedule confirmation, estimator ETA notifications, digital design proofs, and critical installation logistics.
  • Frequency: Typically 2–4 text messages per booked project phase. We do not blast recurring automated marketing texts.
  • Message & Data Rates: Standard mobile carrier rates may apply depending on your provider plan.
  • Opt-Out Command: You may reply STOP to any SMS at any time to immediately cancel all future text messages. Reply HELP for immediate staff assistance.
manage_accounts Section 08

8. Homeowner Rights & Opt-Out Requests

Regardless of whether your project is active, completed, or in preliminary estimation, you retain full sovereignty over your consumer records:

Right to Inspect

Request a digital summary copy of all measurements, architectural notes, and billing history attached to your property.

Right to Erasure

Request deletion of uncontracted lead profiles or outdated contact records (subject only to state tax and warranty retention laws).

Right to Correction

Update or rectify inaccurate primary phone numbers, emails, or changed address coordinates anytime.

No Retaliatory Bias

We will never alter remodeling pricing, warranty terms, or service commitments because you exercise your privacy rights.
