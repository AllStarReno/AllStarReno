Section 08

Homeowner Rights & Opt-Out Requests

Every homeowner maintains full authority over their personal contact records:

1. Right to Inspect

Request an itemized summary of personal data and project notes held in your customer account file.

2. Right to Erasure

Request complete deletion of marketing and contact records (excluding active warranty registrations and building code compliance documents).

3. Right to Correction

Promptly rectify inaccurate telephone numbers, addresses, or warranty ownership information.

4. No Retaliatory Bias

We never change estimates, quote terms, or labor quality if you choose to exercise your privacy preferences.
