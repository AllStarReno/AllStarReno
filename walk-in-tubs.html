Walk-In Tub vs Walk-In Shower

Both options improve safety and accessibility. A walk-in shower is typically faster to install, easier to maintain, and more popular for everyday use. A walk-in tub is better if you specifically want the therapeutic benefits of soaking. During your free consultation we help you choose the option that fits your needs, lifestyle, and budget.

Our Installation Approach

We evaluate your existing bathroom, confirm plumbing requirements, and install a properly fitted walk-in tub with attention to waterproofing, access, and finish details. Many projects can be completed in a short timeframe so your bathroom is back in service quickly.
