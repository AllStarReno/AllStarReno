The 48-Hour Installation Process

Our licensed Arkansas plumbing specialists execute an uncompromised military-precision sequence designed to protect your home's structure while ensuring flawless water-containment.

DAY 1 • 08:00 – 12:00 01

Surgical Tear-Out & Plumbing

We remove old cast iron or fiberglass tubs without disturbing adjoining drywall. Copper/PEX water lines are re-routed and copper drain traps are aligned to laser level.

check_circle Subfloor rot inspection complete
DAY 1 • 13:00 – 17:00 02

Sub-Pan Anchor & Moisture Seal

Reinforced structural sub-base is bedded into commercial mortar. We install slip-resistant, non-porous composite pans with pre-pitched 1/4" drainage grades.

check_circle 100% leak-proof hydrostatic check
DAY 2 • 08:00 – 12:30 03

Direct-to-Stud RCB Wall Panels

Solid stone-composite slabs are laser-cut and anchored directly to the framing. Tongue-and-groove mechanical joints lock flush with zero exposed grout lines.

check_circle Antimicrobial nano-coating
DAY 2 • 13:00 – 16:30 04

Tempered Glass & Final Cure

Installation of 3/8" heavy architectural glass enclosures, Moen/Delta valve fixtures, grab bars, and marine-grade silicone. Ready for use the following morning.
