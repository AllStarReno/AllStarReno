Material Cost Realities: Ceramic Tile vs. Cheap Acrylic vs. Rigid RCB Composite

When evaluating remodeling proposals, Arkansas homeowners are often confused by quotes that range from $4,000 to $22,000 for what appears to be the same space. The primary cost divergence lies in wall substrates, moisture durability, and labor installation hours.

Material Substrate Average Turnaround Arkansas Installed Cost Maintenance & Mold Risk Warranty Expectation
Traditional Hand-Laid Tile 7 to 14 Business Days $12,000 – $24,000 High. Porous cement grout absorbs moisture and fosters black mold; requires annual sealing. 1–3 Years Contractor Warranty
Thin Glued Acrylic Liners 1 Day $4,000 – $7,000 Moderate. Can flex or hollow out behind the wall; discoloration from harsh Arkansas tap minerals. 5–10 Years (Prorated)
verified All-Star RCB Marble Panels 1 to 2 Days $5,000 – $11,500 Zero Maintenance. 100% solid stone composite core. Zero grout channels. Antimicrobial shield. Lifetime Material & Labor
science

What is RCB? Rigid Core Board (RCB) combines 70% crushed natural stone with specialized stabilization polymers. It possesses the authentic thermal mass, cool touch, and high-definition natural veining of Italian marble, but with extreme structural rigidity that will never bend, yellow, crack, or harbor trapped humidity.

Hidden Costs in Central Arkansas Homes (And How We Eliminate Them)

Older housing stock across Central Arkansas—from mid-century ranches in Little Rock’s Heights and Midtown neighborhoods to 1980s homes in Conway's Sherwood Park and Centennial Valley—present distinct architectural idiosyncrasies that inexperienced contractors overlook:

water_damage Subfloor Moisture Rot

Slow leaks around aging cast iron bathtub overflow channels frequently rot plywood joists. While rogue handymen tile over wet timber, All-Star technicians inspect and sister framing before enclosing any wall.

plumbing Galvanized Drain Transitions

Tub drains use 1.5-inch lines, whereas modern walk-in shower codes require a 2-inch rapid drain line. We transition your plumbing all the way to the cleanout to prevent standing water backups.

assignment_turned_in Municipal Code Compliance

Permit enforcement across Conway, Little Rock, and Maumelle requires licensed oversight. Our Arkansas Contractor License #103829 ensures every valve, shutoff, and safety bar passes municipal codes.
