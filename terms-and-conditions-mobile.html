03

Installation & 1–2 Day Protocols

3.1 Standard 1–2 Day Wet-Area Turnaround: Alcove conversions and panel replacements are engineered for completion within 1 to 2 consecutive operational days to minimize residential plumbing disruptions.

3.2 Hidden Pre-Existing Conditions: Timelines apply exclusively to structurally sound envelopes. Discovery of structural wet rot, subfloor sag, active termite infestations, or non-code vent stacks triggers an immediate work pause until a formal Change Order is authorized.

3.3 Jobsite Access: Homeowner guarantees unobstructed pathing from 7:30 AM to 5:00 PM CST, standard utility water shut-off access, and climate-controlled curing between 65°F and 80°F.

04

Lifetime Warranties & Workmanship

4.1 Rigid Core Board (RCB) Lifetime Material Warranty: Panels are warranted for the natural lifetime of the purchaser against surface delamination, bubbling, water permeation, and ultraviolet yellowing under ordinary residential care.

4.2 Zero-Grout Guarantee: Non-porous interlocking surfaces require no cementitious grout lines, eliminating traditional mold propagation zones and cracking hazards.

workspace_premium Five-Year Workmanship Accord

Arkansas 3-Day Right of Rescission

assignment_late STATUTORY CANCELLATION NOTICE

Under Arkansas Code Title 4 and Federal Trade Commission door-to-door solicitation protections, you have the absolute legal right to cancel any residential contract within three (3) business days of signing, without penalty, prior to midnight of the third business day.

Cancellations initiated after the statutory period where custom fabricated wall assemblies or custom door dimensions have been ordered are subject to restocking costs not to exceed 15% of the material deposit.
